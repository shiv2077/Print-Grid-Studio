# PrintGrid Studio — Design Contract

Authoritative spec for visual + interaction design. Every component, page,
copy choice must comply. Violations = revert and rebuild.

Reference vibes (in order of importance):
1. onlyscrews.in — focused Indian D2C, plain copy, "anywhere in India in 2 days"
2. jlc3dp.com — industrial manufacturing aesthetic, instant-quote workflow
3. Vintage HP / Tektronix instrument manuals — title blocks, dimension callouts, hairlines
4. Bambu Lab product pages — clinical, technical confidence
5. Dieter Rams 10 principles — function first

## Aesthetic adjectives
editorial · industrial · technical · restrained · Indian · honest · no-bullshit

## Aesthetic anti-adjectives (reject on sight)
corporate · cute · playful · gradient-y · SaaS · Apple-Marcom · web3 · luxury

## Typography

Numbers + technical labels: **JetBrains Mono** (weights 400, 500)
